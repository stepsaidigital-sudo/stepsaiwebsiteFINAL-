PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Ten building blocks. Every industry combines them differently.

Real estate leans on property discovery and site visits. A law firm leans on intake and conflict screening. Underneath, it's the same ten capabilities, wired for the job in front of them.

01 The building blocks

Ten capabilities. Every deployment uses a different mix.

01 · Knowledge Q&A

Answers approved questions from websites, documents, FAQs, policies, catalogues, programmes, properties, services, or internal systems.

02 · Lead or request capture

Collects structured customer information without forcing a long static form.

03 · Intent detection

Recognizes when a visitor is ready to book, buy, apply, visit, consult, or speak with a human.

04 · Cards and recommendations

Shows properties, courses, doctors, rooms, services, or lawyers with clear next actions.

05 · Booking

Books site visits, counselling sessions, medical appointments, hotel services, or legal consultations.

06 · WhatsApp follow-up

Continues the journey after the visitor leaves the website.

07 · Document and image uploads

Collects supporting documents, screenshots, reports, evidence, or identification where permitted.

08 · Ticket creation

Creates internal tasks for unresolved questions, complaints, service requests, or specialist review.

09 · Human handoff

Transfers the complete conversation and structured context to the right team.

10 · Analytics

Tracks enquiry volume, common questions, bookings, lead sources, drop-off, and agent outcomes.

02 WhatsApp workflows

How a one-to-one follow-up actually runs.

The same nine-step logic behind every automated WhatsApp workflow, whatever triggers it.

Trigger Segment Approved message Customer reply AI conversation Action or human handoff Outcome tracking
1 · Capture permission

Collect a clear WhatsApp opt-in through the website, form, booking flow, QR code, or an existing conversation.

2 · Start from a trigger

Launch after a defined event: an enquiry, incomplete application, booking, site visit, or consultation request.

3 · Load the right context

Pull the customer profile, enquiry details, selected service, and the next expected action.

4 · Send the approved message

A concise message with a clear purpose, useful information, and one primary call to action.

5 · Let the AI handle replies

Answer follow-up questions, retrieve approved information, and recommend the next step.

6 · Branch by customer action

Move the customer into the correct path based on reply, click, booking, upload, payment, or opt-out.

7 · Complete an action

Book a meeting, create a lead, share a document, update a ticket, or notify the internal team.

8 · Escalate intent or risk

Transfer high-intent, sensitive, urgent, or unresolved conversations to a human with full context attached.

9 · Record the outcome

Track the response, booking, conversion, handoff, ticket, and next follow-up date.
