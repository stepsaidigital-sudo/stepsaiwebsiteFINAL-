### Section 2 — The six predefined skills
**[Kicker]** 02 Built in, ready before you turn one on
**[H2]** Six skills. Each one has its own job.
**[Body]** Turn on what you need. Every skill is matched by intent, so it only runs when a conversation actually calls for it.

**[Card title]** Book appointment
**[Card body]** Connects to one booking provider you choose per skill, though you can connect as many providers as you need. Collects name, email, company or job title, whichever fields you've turned on, then books the slot directly.

**[Card title]** Customer creation
**[Card body]** Turns a conversation into a CRM record without anyone typing it in twice. Someone asks a question, gets an answer, and they're already saved when you check your CRM later.

**[Card title]** Human handoff
**[Card body]** Waits 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, or 2 minutes, whichever you set, before it passes the conversation to a person. Sends a fallback message so nobody's left staring at nothing, and emails whoever you choose to notify. Leave that list empty and the emails just don't go out.

**[Card title]** Ticket creation
**[Card body]** Opens a ticket with the fields the issue actually needs: text, long text, numbers, email, phone, a dropdown, even a file. Mark any of them required, and email as many people as the issue calls for.

**[Card title]** Web search
**[Card body]** Checks your own content first. It only reaches the web when the answer isn't in what you've given it, so it doesn't go wandering off answering things you never approved.

**[Card title]** Shipment landed cost calculator
**[Card body]** Built for Indian importers and sourcing agents pricing a shipment before it's cleared. Set your own air and sea freight rates, a density surcharge, insurance, banking margin and commission, or leave them blank and it falls back to built-in estimates.

### FAQ
**[Kicker]** 04 Questions
**[H2]** About skills.
**[FAQ Q]** Can I add my own skill?
**[FAQ A]** Yes. Build a custom skill as an AI skill, which is model instructions with trigger examples, or a Flow skill, which collects information from the widget first and then runs an HTTP request or a connected app action.
**[FAQ Q]** What happens when a skill doesn't apply?
**[FAQ A]** Nothing, on purpose. Skills are matched by intent, so a skill only runs when a conversation actually calls for it. The rest of the time your agent just answers from what it already knows.
**[FAQ Q]** Does web search mean it can answer anything from the internet?
**[FAQ A]** No. It checks your own content first and only reaches the web when your knowledge base doesn't cover the question. It won't go looking for information you haven't given it.
**[FAQ Q]** Who is the shipment landed cost calculator actually for?
**[FAQ A]** Indian importers and sourcing agents pricing a shipment before it arrives, not a typical Shopify store. Leave your own freight rates unset and it falls back to built-in estimates.
