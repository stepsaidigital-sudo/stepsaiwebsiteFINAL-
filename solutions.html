SOLUTIONS

One agent. Every role you need it to play.

Marketing, sales, support, and operations all draw on the same agent — the same memory of your products, your customers, and your answers. You don't run four tools. You run one.

BY ROLE

Point it at a job. It already knows how to do it.

Every role below runs on the same underlying agent from the homepage — this is what changes depending on who's using it.

Marketing & Growth

Turns comments, DMs, and site visits into qualified conversations before a human ever sees them.

  • Answers campaign & promo questions instantly
  • Captures leads from every channel into one list
  • Segments and re-engages cold contacts on WhatsApp

Sales

Qualifies who's ready to buy, answers pricing and fit questions, and books the meeting without a rep touching it.

  • Qualifies leads against your ideal customer profile
  • Books meetings straight into your calendar
  • Hands off warm leads to a rep with full context

Support & CX

Resolves the questions you answer every day, and hands over cleanly — with full history — when it can't.

  • Resolves order, account, and product questions
  • Escalates to your team with the full conversation intact
  • Never repeats a question the customer already answered

Operations

Chases the follow-ups your team keeps meaning to get to — abandoned carts, quiet leads, forgotten appointments.

  • Runs automated workflows on a schedule you set
  • Recovers abandoned carts and re-engages quiet leads
  • Reports what it did in plain English, weekly
