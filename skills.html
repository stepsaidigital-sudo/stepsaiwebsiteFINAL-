02 Built in, ready before you turn one on

Six skills. Each one has its own job.

Turn on what you need. Every skill is matched by intent, so it only runs when a conversation actually calls for it.

Book appointment

Connects to one booking provider you choose per skill, though you can connect as many providers as you need. Collects name, email, company or job title, whichever fields you've turned on, then books the slot directly.

Customer creation

Turns a conversation into a CRM record without anyone typing it in twice. Someone asks a question, gets an answer, and they're already saved when you check your CRM later.

Human handoff

Waits 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, or 2 minutes, whichever you set, before it passes the conversation to a person. Sends a fallback message so nobody's left staring at nothing, and emails whoever you choose to notify. Leave that list empty and the emails just don't go out.

Ticket creation

Opens a ticket with the fields the issue actually needs: text, long text, numbers, email, phone, a dropdown, even a file. Mark any of them required, and email as many people as the issue calls for.

Web search

Checks your own content first. It only reaches the web when the answer isn't in what you've given it, so it doesn't go wandering off answering things you never approved.

Shipment landed cost calculator

Built for Indian importers and sourcing agents pricing a shipment before it's cleared. Set your own air and sea freight rates, a density surcharge, insurance, banking margin and commission, or leave them blank and it falls back to built-in estimates.
