Security & Trust

Security and privacy, built in by default.

Your customer data, conversation history, and CRM records belong to you. We don't sell it, and we don't use your conversations to train models for other customers. "Enterprise-grade security" gets said by every company with a login page — below is specifically what we do, and where we're still building toward bigger certifications.

Encryption everywhere

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, using current industry-standard protocols.

Your data isn't training material

Conversations and CRM records that flow through StepsAI aren't used to train models for other customers.

Access, export, or delete — on request

You, and the people who chat with your agent, can ask to access, export, or delete personal data, consistent with our Privacy Policy.

Access controls for your team

Control who on your team can see what, with account-level permissions and secure authentication on every login.

Architecture

How we protect your business and your customers.

What that looks like in practice — across infrastructure, the AI itself, and who gets access to what.

Data Privacy & Isolation

Every business's workspace on StepsAI is kept separate. No other business can see your data, conversations, or CRM records.

  • Request deletion of your account data when you cancel
  • Messages arrive through WhatsApp's official Meta Cloud API, not a workaround
  • We look at usage patterns in aggregate, not individual conversations, to improve the product

AI Safety & Guardrails

StepsAI agents operate within guardrails you set. They answer only from your approved content, and hand off to a human when they're not sure.

  • Filters to catch prompt injection and jailbreak attempts
  • The agent won't invent discounts, policies, or facts that aren't in your content
  • When it isn't confident, it says so and hands off — it doesn't bluff

Infrastructure & Availability

Hosted on established cloud infrastructure, with backups and monitoring so an outage doesn't mean losing your data.

  • Uptime commitments available for Enterprise customers, on request
  • Regular backups, so a bad day doesn't mean losing your data
  • Standard protections against traffic spikes and denial-of-service attempts

Compliance & Vendor Assessments

If your procurement process needs a security questionnaire or a Data Processing Agreement, we'll work through it with you directly.

  • A Data Processing Agreement (DPA), available on request
  • Straight answers on what we do — and don't — have in place yet
  • Support for security reviews as part of your evaluation

Need a security review or a DPA?

We're happy to walk through a security questionnaire, documentation, or a compliance review directly with your team.
