## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners and customer-facing teams evaluating an AI agent for sales, support, lead capture, appointment booking, and customer messaging across their website and major messaging channels.

## Product Purpose

StepsAI helps businesses answer customer questions and take the next useful action inside the same conversation. Success means a visitor receives a grounded answer and can move toward a sale, lead, booking, or resolved support request without unnecessary waiting or handoffs.

## Positioning

StepsAI connects conversations to business context and actions such as product recommendations, order status, CRM capture, and scheduling rather than stopping at a generic text response.

## Operating Context

The product is evaluated through marketing pages and illustrated product demonstrations. It is presented across websites, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, stores, calendars, CRMs, knowledge sources, and workflow examples.
